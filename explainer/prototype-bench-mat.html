Why the black squares?

Each is a different, genuine ArUco marker. Their known positions give a future detector named points for locating the sheet, its rotation and perspective. Spread-out markers can remain visible when an object covers the centre. That is why they live at the edge.

The mat is a proposal; DeskCam does not yet recognise either kind of landmark. At high zoom, no visible marker means no new absolute sheet position. A repeated grid alone cannot say which square is in view.

What this does and does not measure

Letters and numbers address 25 mm cells. Optional interior labels leave a nearby address visible in a close crop, if the object does not cover it. In the grid and quiet versions, A01 is the upper-left cell. Sheet coordinates start at the working field’s upper-left corner; x goes right and y goes down.

The angle version uses 0° up, increasing clockwise. Read angles with the part aligned to the centre, or after correcting the camera view to the paper plane.

Scale applies to the paper plane. A raised component’s top is closer to the camera, so its apparent size is different. Lens calibration is a separate job; this is not a replacement for a calibration board.

Print and camera considerations

Start with flat, matte paper. The fine grid is deliberately quieter than an object edge. Use the stronger option if your printer loses it; making every line heavy can make a small part harder to separate from its background.

The isolated reference bars use identical 5 mm ticks. For the current DeskCam scale tool, select one strip as the reference and specify a 5 mm pitch. The grid and marker patterns are competing periodic textures.

Focus on the object, not a crisp background marking. Keep several edge markers visible when you want to locate the whole sheet. Printed grey is not a calibrated exposure or colour reference.

Sources and design status

The geometry and line weights are design proposals, pending a real home-printer and camera trial. In coded mode, marker patterns are generated from OpenCV’s DICT_4X4_50, IDs 0–7, with 16 mm black outer squares and a 3 mm white surround.
